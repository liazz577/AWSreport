### 1. Overall evaluation of the program

**1. Learning and working environment**
The First Cloud AI Journey program is well organized. The weekly roadmap and clear materials let me track my own progress. Combining self-study, labs and workshops gave me flexibility while still having concrete goals each week.

**2. Support from mentors and admins**
The mentors are enthusiastic and have a lot of real-world experience. When I hit an error, they usually showed me how to read the logs and documentation to find the cause myself instead of handing me the answer, which made the lessons stick. The admins handled paperwork and AWS accounts quickly so I could start hands-on work early.

**3. Fit with my major**
As a Computer Science student, I found the hands-on content closely related to what I study: programming, data structures and system design. Building SnapThumb let me apply Python to a real system on the cloud, from event-driven architecture and a NoSQL database to writing tests and deploying with Infrastructure as Code.

**4. Learning and skill development**
I worked with technologies widely used in industry, such as serverless, Infrastructure as Code (CloudFormation), and monitoring and alerting with CloudWatch. Beyond the technical side, I improved at writing bilingual technical reports and managing my time through weekly worklogs.

**5. Culture and team spirit**
The AWS Study Group community is lively and generous. Interns share the errors they run into and tips for labs, so everyone gets through the hard parts together.

---

### 2. Survey answers

- **What I liked most:** working directly on AWS and turning knowledge into a real product that people can try through a link.
- **What could be improved:** regular weekly small-group check-ins to share common configuration mistakes, so newcomers lose less time.
- **Would I recommend it to friends:** Yes. It is a structured, hands-on cloud program that narrows the gap between university knowledge and what companies need.

---

### 3. Suggestions and wishes

- **Suggestion:** more deep-dive sessions on serverless architecture design and cost optimization, including walkthroughs of projects from previous interns. It would also help to explain early how to check a new account's service quotas, so interns pick a feasible project from the start.
- **Wish:** to keep taking part in AWS Study Group activities to stay up to date and keep learning from mentors and peers.
